Finding the right org shouldn't feel like a scavenger hunt.

Right now, that's exactly what it is — and it's costing students organizations they'd actually want to join.

01

Information is scattered everywhere

Every org runs its own Facebook page, Messenger chat, or Google Form. There's no single place to see what exists, what's open, or what's required.

02

Deadlines get missed

Recruitment posts get buried in a feed within a day. Students who would have applied never even see the announcement.

03

Applicants are left guessing

Once you apply, there's often no way to know if it was received, let alone where you stand — so officers field the same "any update?" messages all week.

Everything about campus organizations, in one app.

OrgConnect brings discovery, applications, and updates together — for students finding their org, and officers running recruitment.

Organization Directory

See every active org on campus, searchable in seconds

No more scrolling through dozens of separate Facebook pages — browse by category, member count, and recruitment status in one directory.

Application Tracker

Know exactly where your application stands

Applied, under review, interview scheduled, accepted — real-time status replaces the waiting and wondering (and the repeated "any update?" DMs).

Events & Reminders

Never miss a deadline or a general assembly again

Every org's events and application deadlines live on one shared calendar, with reminders — not a post you scrolled past three days ago.
